The Challenge

Traditional gaming AI requires millions of labeled examples and game-specific engineering. This doesn't scale.

We asked: Can we learn to play games by watching streamers?

Our Approach

  • check_circle Scrape public gaming streams from the internet
  • check_circle Detect controllers in video frames
  • check_circle Parse button presses automatically
  • check_circle Train vision-language models end-to-end

How It Works

A fully automated pipeline that turns raw gameplay videos into training data

1

Stream Collection

Automatically download gameplay videos from public streaming platforms, filtering for controller overlays.

2

Controller Detection

A custom YOLO model locates and crops the controller overlay from each frame with 98.7% accuracy.

3

Action Parsing

A specialized SegFormer model reads the cropped controller to determine which buttons are pressed in every frame.
