The "Big Idea": Gamepad Overlays

Most gaming videos lack action labels. NitroGen solves this by targeting videos where streamers overlay their controller state, then training a vision model to "watch" the controller.

1

Raw Video Collection

The system scans YouTube for high-quality gameplay videos that contain a "gamepad overlay" visual.

2

Overlay Extraction

A template matching algorithm identifies the controller's position and crops it from the frame.

3

Action Parsing

A specialized SegFormer model reads the cropped controller to determine which buttons are pressed in every frame.

Why Behavior Cloning?

Unlike Reinforcement Learning (RL) which requires a specific reward function for every game, NitroGen uses Behavior Cloning (BC) to imitate human players from the massive video dataset. This allows it to scale to 1000+ games without needing game-specific code.

Performance & Fine-Tuning

NitroGen serves as a "Foundation Model". It can play many games zero-shot, but its real power is how efficiently it adapts to new games compared to training from scratch.
